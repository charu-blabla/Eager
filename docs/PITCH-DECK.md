## Slide 1 — Title

# EAGER
**Know you want to build. Not sure what.**

A personalized project idea generator for students building their portfolio.

---

## Slide 2 — The Problem

### "I want to build something" isn't the hard part.
### Deciding WHAT to build is.

1. **Time wasted deciding** — Hours lost browsing lists instead of building.
2. **Mismatched difficulty** — Too ambitious → abandoned. Too easy → no growth, weak portfolio signal.
3. **Not portfolio-ready** — Finished projects that don't read well on a resume or GitHub.
4. **No structure once chosen** — Uncertainty about folders, scope, and how to plan the build.

---

## Slide 3 — Target Users

### Built for students building their portfolio.

**The Portfolio-Building Student**
- Comfortable with at least one language/stack, actively building a portfolio
- Has a defined, limited time window — wants to spend it building, not deciding
- Values proof-of-work: live projects, clean repos, resume-ready lines
- Learns by exploring — uses AI as a thinking partner, not just a search engine

> "I don't need more tutorials. I need a project that fits where I actually am right now."

---

## Slide 4 — The Solution

### Eager matches you to the right project — then makes it yours.

1. **Tell Eager about you** — Skill level, tech stack, time available
2. **Browse matched ideas** — Curated bank, filtered to fit — not generic
3. **Get a personalized brief** — AI-tailored features, folder structure, roadmap, resume line
4. **Star it, come back anytime** — Favorites saved right in your browser

---

## Slide 5 — Key Features

- **Smart Matching** — Filters a 40-idea bank by skill level, stack, and time budget
- **AI Personalization** — Every idea expands into a brief tailored to you, not a generic template
- **Folder Structure** — A ready-to-use project scaffold, sized to your chosen stack
- **Learning Roadmap** — Week-by-week plan that fits your actual available time
- **Resume Description** — A one-line, resume-ready summary generated with the idea
- **Favorites & Share** — Star ideas to revisit anytime; share a link to any expanded brief

---

## Slide 6 — Technical Approach

### Curated quality, personalized by AI.
*Not a raw AI generator. Not a static list. A hybrid of both.*

**Curated Idea Bank**
- 40 hand-picked ideas — 10 each across Web, Mobile, AI/ML, DSA
- Quality-controlled, not randomly generated
- Reliable foundation the AI layer builds on top of

**AI Personalization Layer**
- Tailors each idea to skill level, stack, and time
- Generates features, folder structure, roadmap, resume line
- Powered by the Anthropic API, called through a serverless proxy
